# Incident playbook

1. Receive latency, error-rate, log, trace, or infrastructure evidence.
2. Detect a statistical anomaly or correlated alert threshold.
3. Create or attach an incident.
4. Review timeline and correlated evidence.
5. Review root-cause candidates and recommendations.
6. Apply only approved remediation actions.
7. Verify latency and error-rate recovery.
8. Move the incident to `RESOLVED`, then `CLOSED`.
9. Export the incident report and record follow-up actions.
